Mass properties

Method

Units are mm, g and g/cm³. Each part is a parametric primitive whose volume, area, center of mass and inertia tensor come from closed-form formulas, then are rotated (R·I·Rᵀ) and combined with the parallel-axis theorem. Imported STL meshes are integrated triangle by triangle (divergence theorem) and must be watertight. All of it is checked against textbook answers; the faceted display mesh is shown against the exact value in the math drawer.

Limits: primitives only, no booleans, fillets or sketches yet. Overlapping parts are counted twice. Hidden parts are left out of mass properties and STL export. The bounding box comes from the display mesh. Densities are nominal handbook values.
